Privacy Policy

Effective 26 November 2026 · Kesitics Studios

The short version

  • We collect what the app needs to plan and price your meals.
  • No ads in the app, and we never sell your data. On iPhone, only if you tap “Allow” when asked, the app tells TikTok about a few steps (install, setup, trial, purchase) so we can see which of our TikTok ads work. Say no and nothing is sent.
  • Your diet and allergy answers are used only to keep the wrong foods out of your meals.
  • You can delete your account and everything saved to it from inside the app, at any time.

Who we are

WisprFood is an app for iPhone and Android that plans a week of meals and prices them at the supermarket you choose. It is run by Kesitics Studios ("we"), the data controller for the information described here. You can reach us at info@wisprfood.com.

Weekly store deals (US, Canada and UK)

The app can show you what's on sale at your supermarket this week and which of your meals are cheaper. In the US and Canada, weekly ads change from place to place, so if you give us your ZIP or postal code we use it to find your shop's nearest stores and the deals there. Once a week our server looks up the deals for your shop and code. It sends only the shop and the ZIP or postal code to Kroger's product service and to Apify, which reads the shop's public weekly ad; it never sends your name, email, account or anything else about you. UK offers are the same across the country, so no postcode is used for them: Apify reads the shop's public offers once a week for everyone. We don't use your device's location (GPS). You can change or remove your code at any time in Profile, and it is deleted with your account.

Diet and allergy information

Allergies and diets can reveal health information, which data-protection law treats with extra care. We ask for them only so the app never plans a meal with something you've told us to avoid, and we use them for nothing else. By entering them you give us your explicit consent to use them this way. You can change or remove them at any time in Profile, and they are deleted with your account.

WisprFood is not a medical service. Always check labels: recipes and ingredient information may contain mistakes.

Fridge photos

When you scan your fridge, the photo is sent to our server and on to Anthropic, whose AI model identifies the ingredients in it. We do not store the photo: once the list of ingredients comes back, it is discarded. Anthropic processes it as our service provider and does not use it to train its models.

What stays on your phone

Some things never leave your phone: which meals you've marked cooked or skipped, your under-budget streak, recipes you import, your language, and when the app last asked you for a review. A reminder before your free trial ends is scheduled on your phone if you allow notifications. Your country is suggested from your phone's region setting, which is read on the phone.

Payments

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through the App Store on iPhone, and by Google through Google Play on Android. We never see your card or payment details; the app only learns from Apple or Google whether your subscription is active.

Measuring our ads (iPhone, only if you allow it)

We advertise WisprFood on TikTok. To learn which ads bring people to the app, the iPhone app includes TikTok's App Events SDK, which runs only if you tap “Allow” when your iPhone asks whether WisprFood may track you. If you allow it, the app sends TikTok your device's advertising identifier, basic device details (model, iOS version, language and IP address) and these events: the app was installed or opened, setup was finished, an account was created, a free trial was started, and a subscription or purchase was made (with the product and its price). We never send TikTok your name, email, account ID, household, diet or allergy answers, or your meals. TikTok uses this to measure and improve our ad campaigns, under its own privacy policy (tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy).

If you don't allow tracking, the app sends nothing to TikTok. Apple's own SKAdNetwork may still tell TikTok, anonymously and without any information about you, that an install came from one of its ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. The Android app and the website don't use this.

Who processes your data for us

  • Supabase hosts your account and the data above, in the European Union.
  • Google and Apple, if you sign in with them, confirm who you are.
  • Anthropic identifies the ingredients in fridge photos, as described above. Anthropic is based in the United States; the transfer is covered by safeguards recognised under EU law.
  • Apple and Google handle subscriptions and payments, through the App Store and Google Play.
  • Kroger and Apify (both in the United States) receive a shop and, in the US and Canada, a ZIP or postal code, with nothing about you, to look up that shop's weekly deals, as described above.
  • PostHog (servers in the European Union) records how the app is used during setup and on the subscription screen, including recordings of those screens with everything you type hidden, so we can make them clearer. Recording stops once you have a plan.
  • TikTok receives the ad-measurement events described above, on iPhone, only if you allow tracking.

We do not sell your data. The only thing shared for advertising is the TikTok ad measurement above, and only if you allow it.

This website

wisprfood.com is hosted by GitHub Pages. To deliver the pages, GitHub processes your IP address and keeps it in its server logs. The website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. The typeface is loaded from Google Fonts, which also receives your IP address when a page loads.

The legal basis

We use your account, household and plan information because you ask us to provide the app (performance of a contract). Diet and allergy information is used with your explicit consent. Limiting the number of fridge scans relies on our legitimate interest in keeping the service available for everyone. Ad measurement with TikTok relies on your consent, given through your iPhone’s tracking prompt, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings. Understanding how setup and the subscription screen are used relies on our legitimate interest in improving the app.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. When you delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account), your account and everything saved to it are deleted straight away. Copies in our hosting provider's backups are overwritten within its backup cycle, at most 30 days later.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete or export your information, to restrict or object to how we use it, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Most of this you can do yourself in the app; for anything else, write to info@wisprfood.com and we will reply within one month. If you live in the European Union or the UK, you also have the right to complain to the data-protection authority where you live (in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, aepd.es).

If you live in the United States

Depending on your state (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia or Texas), you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to get a copy of it, to have it corrected or deleted, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. To use them, delete your account in the app or write to info@wisprfood.com from the email on your account; someone you authorize can also ask for you. If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer.

We do not sell your personal information. If you allow tracking on iPhone, the ad-measurement events described above are shared with TikTok, which some state laws treat as “sharing” for advertising. You can opt out at any time by turning tracking off for WisprFood in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, or by writing to info@wisprfood.com. Your diet and allergy answers are sensitive information under some state laws; we use them only to plan meals you can eat, as described above, and for nothing else.

Children

WisprFood is meant to be used by adults planning meals for their household. It is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Children in your household are only counted, so their meals can be sized; we don't ask for their names or ages.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

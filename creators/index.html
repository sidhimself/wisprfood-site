Your first video

The test video

We ask every creator for one short test video, around 30 seconds, so we can check we're aligned on the format before going further. We'll send you the app and tell you where to drop it.

How to film it

How long

Around 30 seconds.

The hook

Start with a 2-second genuine reaction, looking straight at the camera: surprised, impressed, "wait, what?". Add a written hook on screen, Snapchat style. For example: "POV: I set a $50 budget and an app planned my whole week".

Then the screen recording, starting on the ZIP code

The recording starts on the ZIP code screen. Then pick one of the most popular shops, Walmart or Aldi, and go through the whole setup until your week is ready.

Same settings every time
  • Cooking for 2 people.
  • Dinners only.
  • Weekly budget around $50.
When your week is ready, 3 key moments
  1. Scroll through the week, tap a recipe, show it, and show you can swap it for another.
  2. Show the week is under your budget.
  3. Show the shopping list, priced at your shop.
Filming again

To start from the first screen for a new video, go to Profile → Delete account in the creator version of the app. It starts fresh every time.

Keep it real

Show the real app and the real prices on screen. Never make up how much you saved.

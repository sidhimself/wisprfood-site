How it works

Your week, planned in two minutes.

Answer a few questions about your table, your shop and your budget. WisprFood plans every meal, prices it at your supermarket and writes one grocery list.

Plan my week

How it works

  1. 1

    Tell us who's eating

    Who's eating, what each person can't eat, your budget, your shop and the kitchen you've got. A vegetarian and a six-year-old can still share one dinner. It takes about two minutes.

  2. 2

    Get your week

    Every meal for the days you cook, priced at your shop. Swap anything you don't feel like and the total updates.

  3. 3

    Shop once, then cook

    One grocery list sorted by aisle. At the stove, cooking mode takes you through each step with timers built in.

New

Your shop's weekly ad, matched to your meals

WisprFood reads this week's ad at your supermarket and checks it against your plan. If chicken thighs are on offer and they're already in Thursday's dinner, you'll see it, along with what you save.

  • In your week: offers on things you're already cooking, with the saving on each.
  • One tap adds a deal to your grocery list.
  • Open a product for nutrition facts from its label, or typical values for that kind of food.
  • In the US and Canada, add your ZIP or postal code to get the ad for your area.

What the same week costs at other shops

WisprFood prices your whole week at each supermarket in your country. If another shop is cheaper, you'll see by how much. Your shop stays your choice, and the plan follows it.

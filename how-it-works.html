How it works

Your week, planned in three steps.

  1. 1

    Tell us about your table

    People, diets, allergies, budget, your shop and the kitchen you have. It takes about two minutes.

  2. 2

    Get your week

    Every meal for the days you cook, priced at your shop. Swap anything you don't fancy and the total updates.

  3. 3

    Shop once, cook calmly

    One grocery list sorted by aisle, then step-by-step cooking mode with built-in timers.

Plan my week

One plan for the whole table

Meals that suit everyone at your table.

Tell WisprFood who's eating and it plans around all of them, without cooking three different dinners.

  • Per-person swaps. Vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian and allergies, handled inside the same recipe.
  • Smaller portions for kids. The shopping list buys exactly what's needed.
  • Only the days you cook. Breakfast, lunch and dinner, or just dinner.
  • Made for your kitchen. No oven? No air fryer? Recipes follow what you have.

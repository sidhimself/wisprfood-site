FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to download anything?

No. WisprFood works in your browser on any phone, tablet or computer. There is also an iPhone app (Android is coming soon), and the same account works everywhere.

Is it free to try?

Yes. Sign in, set up your week and see your first meal for free, no card needed. Unlock the whole week with Monthly or Lifetime, paid once.

Do I have to shop at the cheapest store?

No. You pick your favourite shop and every price follows it. The shop comparison is a tip, never a rule.

Which countries and shops are covered?

82 supermarkets in 10 countries: the United States, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Spain, France, Germany, Denmark, Poland, Mexico and Colombia.

Can one plan handle different diets and allergies?

Yes. Each person can be vegetarian, vegan or pescatarian and have their own allergies. WisprFood swaps ingredients per person and gives children smaller portions.

How exact are the prices?

They are estimates for each ingredient at each shop and get better over time. They're made for planning a budget, so your receipt may differ a little.

How do I cancel or delete my account?

Cancel any time from your profile, or from App Store or Google Play subscriptions if you subscribed there. Profile → Delete account removes your account and everything saved to it.

How it works

A whole week of meals, planned in a minute.

Answer a few questions once. WisprFood does the planning, the math and the grocery list every week after.

  1. Step 1

    Tell us about your table

    How many adults and kids, which days you cook, your weekly budget, diets, allergies, the proteins you skip and the store you shop at. It takes about a minute.

  2. Step 2

    Get your week, priced

    Breakfast, lunch and dinner on a rolling week, every meal priced at your store for the exact number of people you cook for. Swap, skip or fill any slot with one tap.

  3. Step 3

    See where it's cheapest

    Compare your whole week across the grocery stores near you and see exactly how much you'd save by switching. Then shop one list, sorted by aisle.

  4. Step 4

    Cook it step by step

    Full-screen steps with big text, built-in timers and the right amounts for your household. No scrolling with floury hands.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is WisprFood?

WisprFood is a meal planning app for iPhone, iPad and Android. It plans breakfast, lunch and dinner for everyone in your household, shows what each meal costs at the grocery store you choose, and turns the week into one grocery list sorted by aisle.

Where can I download WisprFood?

WisprFood is on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, and on Google Play for Android phones. Every new account starts with a 3-day free trial.

How does WisprFood know grocery prices?

Every ingredient has a price at every supported store: 70 grocery stores across 10 countries. Meal prices are worked out from the exact amounts in each recipe for the people you cook for. Prices are estimates for planning, so your receipt can differ a little.

Which grocery stores does WisprFood support?

Seven stores in each of our 10 countries, 70 in all, including Walmart in the US, Tesco in the UK, and Aldi, Lidl, Carrefour and Mercadona in Europe. You can compare your whole week across the stores in your country.

Does it work with allergies and diets?

Yes. Tell WisprFood about diets, allergies, proteins you avoid and the equipment in your kitchen. Meals are planned around them, and ingredient swaps can be set per person. Kids get smaller portions, and the grocery list follows.

How much does WisprFood cost?

WisprFood starts with a 3-day free trial. After that it is $44.99 a year (about $3.75 a month) or $8.99 a month in the US. Subscriptions are handled by the App Store or Google Play, and you can cancel any time in your subscription settings.

What happens to my data?

You sign in with Apple or Google, your account data is stored securely in the EU under GDPR, and you can delete your account and everything in it from Profile in the app. Read the privacy policy.

Are the nutrition figures exact?

Calories and macros are rounded grocery-store averages. They are good for planning a balanced week, but they are not medical advice.

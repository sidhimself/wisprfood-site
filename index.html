Every ingredient for the week, sorted by aisle, in the same amounts as the recipes. Check things off as you shop. You'll see the week's total next to your budget before you leave the house.

  • Share it with whoever does the shopping.

What the same week costs at other shops

WisprFood prices your whole week at each supermarket in your country. If another shop is cheaper, you'll see by how much. Your shop stays your choice, and the plan follows it.

Common questions

Is it free to try?

Yes. Sign in, set up your week and see it with one meal open, free and with no card. For the whole week, choose Monthly, or Lifetime, which you pay for once.

Do I have to shop at the cheapest store?

No. You pick your favourite shop and every price follows it. The shop comparison is a tip, never a rule.

Which countries and shops are covered?

Over 100 supermarkets in 12 countries: the United States, Canada, Mexico, Colombia, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Spain, France, Germany, Denmark, Poland and Australia.

Can one plan handle different diets and allergies?

Yes. Each person can be vegetarian, vegan or pescatarian and have their own allergies. WisprFood swaps ingredients per person and gives children smaller portions.

How exact are the prices?

They are estimates for each ingredient at each shop and get better over time. They're made for planning a budget, so your receipt may differ a little.

Where do the deals come from?

From your shop's weekly ad, which we read every week. In the US and Canada we use your ZIP or postal code, because the ads change from area to area. We're adding shops one by one, and some offers need the shop's loyalty card.

How do I cancel or delete my account?

Cancel any time from your profile, or from App Store or Google Play subscriptions if you subscribed there. Profile → Delete account removes your account and everything saved to it.

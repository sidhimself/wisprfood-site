Delete your WisprFood account

WisprFood: Meal Plan & Save, by Kesitics Studios. You can do it in the app, on the web, or by email.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open WisprFood and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Tap Delete my account to confirm.

Your account and everything saved to it are deleted straight away.

On the web

  1. Go to wisprfood.com/app on any computer or phone and sign in with the same Google account you use in the app.
  2. Open Profile, then tap Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete my account to confirm.

This deletes the same account as the app, straight away.

By email

Write to info@wisprfood.com from the email address you sign in with, with the subject “Delete my account”. We delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner, and write back to confirm. If you write from another address, we may ask you to show that the account is yours first.

What is deleted

  • Your account: your name, email address and sign-in link with Google or Apple
  • Your household: the people at your table, their diets, allergies and foods they don't eat, your country, shop and budget
  • Your meal plans, grocery lists, saved recipes and favourites
  • The log of your fridge scans
  • On the phone you delete from: the reminders and the recipes you imported, which are kept only on that phone

What is kept, and for how long

  • Copies in our hosting provider's backups are overwritten within its backup cycle, at most 30 days after you delete.
  • Records of purchases are kept by Google Play or the App Store, and by our subscription provider, because the law requires payment records to be kept for tax and accounting. They contain no meal or household data.
  • Usage statistics from setup and the subscription screen are kept by our analytics provider, PostHog. If you want those deleted too, say so in your email and we will delete them.

Your subscription

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription, because Google or Apple bills it. Cancel it first:

  • Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → WisprFood → Cancel
  • iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → WisprFood → Cancel

To delete only some of your data, such as allergies or a household member, change it in Profile, or write to info@wisprfood.com. More in our privacy policy.
